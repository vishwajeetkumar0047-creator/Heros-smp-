SERVER RULES

Rules & Guidelines

Please follow these rules to keep our server safe, fair and enjoyable for everyone.

01

General Rules

Basic rules every player must follow.

01

Respect Everyone

Treat all players, staff members and community members with respect.

02

No Toxicity

Harassment, bullying, excessive toxicity and personal attacks are not allowed.

03

Follow Staff

Follow reasonable instructions given by server staff.

04

No Rule Evasion

Do not attempt to bypass punishments or server restrictions.

02

Chat Rules

Keep the server chat clean and friendly.

01

No Spam

Do not spam messages, commands, characters or repeated content.

02

No Advertising

Advertising other servers, websites or communities without permission is prohibited.

03

No Abuse

Insults, hate speech and inappropriate language directed at others are not allowed.

04

No False Information

Do not intentionally spread misleading information about the server or its players.

03

Gameplay Rules

Play fairly and respect other players.

01

Play Fair

Use legitimate gameplay methods and do not gain unfair advantages.

02

No Exploiting

Do not abuse bugs, glitches or unintended server mechanics.

03

Respect Builds

Do not intentionally destroy or interfere with another player's builds.

04

No Unfair Traps

Do not create traps designed to unfairly exploit or harass other players.

04

PvP Rules

Keep PvP fair and enjoyable.

01

No Unwanted PvP

Do not repeatedly attack players who clearly do not want to participate in PvP.

02

No Combat Abuse

Do not abuse server mechanics to gain an unfair advantage during combat.

03

No Spawn Killing

Do not repeatedly target players immediately after they enter the server.

04

Fair Fights

Keep PvP competitive and avoid exploiting unfair advantages.

05

Griefing & Stealing

Protect other players and their property.

01

No Griefing

Do not destroy, modify or damage another player's property without permission.

02

No Stealing

Taking items from another player's storage without permission is prohibited.

03

No Property Damage

Do not intentionally damage farms, bases, builds or other player-owned areas.

04

Respect Claims

Respect protected areas and property belonging to other players.

06

Exploiting & Cheating

Any unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.

01

No Hacked Clients

Hacked clients or modifications that provide unfair advantages are not allowed.

02

No Automation

Do not use unauthorized bots, scripts or automation to gain an advantage.

03

No Bug Abuse

Report serious bugs to staff instead of using them for personal benefit.

04

No Duplication

Item duplication glitches or exploits must not be used or distributed.

07

Punishments

Breaking the rules may result in punishment.

01

Warnings

Minor violations may result in a warning from the staff team.

02

Mute

Chat-related violations may result in temporary or permanent chat restrictions.

03

Temporary Ban

Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary ban.

04

Permanent Ban

Severe violations or repeated offenses may result in a permanent ban.
