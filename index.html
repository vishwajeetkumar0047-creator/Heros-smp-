Heros SMP Server

Survival

Our SMP server is fully-equipped with a custom resource pack and tons of completely custom features for you to enjoy. Every part of the server is carefully thought out and we release updates weekly!

Balanced Economy

Our server features a carefully balanced economy where every player has a fair chance to earn and grow. Earn money through jobs, trading, shops, and gameplay while keeping prices and rewards stable. No excessive inflation or unfair advantages—just a competitive economy designed for long-term survival and progression.

Anti-Cheat

Our advanced anti-cheat system keeps HEROS SMP fair and secure for everyone. It detects hacks, cheats, and unfair advantages in real time, helping protect players and maintain a competitive environment. Play confidently, knowing that skill matters—not cheats.

Competitive

Challenge yourself and compete with other players on HEROS SMP. Climb the leaderboards, build your wealth, win intense PvP battles, and prove your skills. Every fight, achievement, and victory brings you closer to becoming one of the strongest players on the server.
